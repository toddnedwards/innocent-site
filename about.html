About Us

The Innocent are an alternative indie-folk act originally hailing from Oswestry, Shropshire. If you have ever been to Oswestry, you will understand why the majority of their songs are in a minor key.

The band started life in 2010, playing original music and garnering praise for their exquisite harmonies and evocative and mesmerising live sets. Since then, they have toured across Europe and America and have supported some of their personal heroes in the alt-folk world such as Gregory Alan Isakov.

They continue to live in Shropshire and North Wales where they enjoy referring to themselves in the third person.

Our Music

Live Music

Based in Shropshire and North Wales, The Innocent are one of the most popular and experienced acoustic acts in the country, covering a wide range of styles and genres, as well as producing their own original music.

When not writing and performing their own music, they can be found putting their own unique spin on classic songs from every genre. As well as regularly performing for private events, their former clients include performances and recordings for the likes of Disney, Radio 5, Ikea and Carnival Cruise Lines.
