Live Music & Bookings

Based in Shropshire and North Wales, The Innocent are one of the most popular and experienced acoustic acts in the country, covering a wide range of styles and genres, as well as producing their own original music.

When not writing and performing their own music, they can be found putting their own unique spin on classic songs from every genre. As well as regularly performing for private events, their former clients include performances and recordings for the likes of Disney, Radio 5, Ikea and Carnival Cruise Lines.

Listen To Our Covers



The Innocent are available as an original act, performing their own evocative indie-folk music, but can also cater for functions, covering a wide range of styles and genres for all events. For original bookings, email: info@theinnocent.co.uk

For functions and corporate events, they offer a range of line-ups including duos, trios and quartets featuring cello, ukulele, piano and/or drums, as well as a four-piece party-band option with a DJ service available on request.

Our Experience

With over a decade of experience performing across Europe and the UK, we have earned a reputation for delivering exceptional live performances for every occasion.

From intimate weddings in country houses to large corporate events, we tailor our performance to create the perfect atmosphere for your event.
